## Curation Note

The earliest launch in this dataset and, per multiple sources (NLB's Singapore history archive, secondary retrospectives), **the very first BTO exercise HDB ever ran** — launched 15 April 2001 as a pilot scheme, described as 'about 2,500 new flats at four sites at Sengkang and Sembawang', before BTO was fully rolled out to replace the older Registration for Flats System in January 2002. Predates all classification frameworks used elsewhere in this dataset. Two Sengkang projects are confirmed with exact totals matching housingmap.sg via current real-estate listings, geocoded normally: Compassvale Arcadia (961 units, 4-Room and 5-Room, split unrecoverable) and Rivervale Green (776 units, 4-Room and 5-Room, split unrecoverable). The two Sembawang projects, Flowing Greenery (410 units per housingmap.sg) and River Edge (447 units per housingmap.sg), could **not be geocoded at all** — neither resolves in OneMap under any name variant tried (second and third null-geocode cases in this backfill, after Dec 2004's Anthias/Coral Green). For River Edge specifically, one secondary source explicitly notes 'River Edge 2001 shown on Wikipedia as built, does not exist in real life', suggesting this pilot-scheme project may have been cancelled or never actually constructed — a real possibility for an early BTO pilot exercise, since HDB's BTO system only proceeds to construction if a site receives sufficient subscription. No pricing, floor area, exact application-close date, or per-type unit split was recoverable for any of the four projects. This represents the practical frontier of this backfill: the entire housingmap.sg roadmap back to April 2001 has now been worked through, with progressively thinner sourcing exactly as anticipated when the user chose to continue at full rigor into pre-2010 territory. **UPDATE**: housingmap.sg's own listing explicitly marks BOTH Flowing Greenery AND River Edge as 'Cancelled' (confirmed via direct re-query of their page), upgrading the earlier hedge from 'may have been cancelled' to a confirmed fact for both Sembawang projects — neither was ever built, which is exactly why neither resolves in OneMap. Compassvale Arcadia and Rivervale Green are both marked 'Completed April 2005' on the same page, consistent with them being fully built and geocodable. housingmap.sg's page also states 'Build-To-Order was introduced in April 2001' verbatim, and directly confirms these four launches are the earliest entries on their entire roadmap — there is nothing earlier. This closes out the full historical backfill: every launch on housingmap.sg's roadmap from April 2001 through the present has now been worked through in this dataset.
