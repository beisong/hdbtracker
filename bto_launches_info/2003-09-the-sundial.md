## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Three projects launched together on 4 Sep 2003 across Punggol, Sengkang and Sembawang, applications closing 24 Sep 2003 (three-week window, confirmed via secondary coverage referencing the NLB NewspaperSG archive index). The Sundial (Punggol, all 4-Room, 903 units, indicative minimum price $138,000) and The Coris (Sengkang, all 4-Room, 448 units, indicative minimum price $157,000) both reconcile exactly to housingmap.sg's totals; only the minimum price was recoverable for either (partial-data pattern, price_max null). Spring Lodge (Sembawang, 4-Room and 5-Room, split unrecoverable) has a confusing history — housingmap.sg's roadmap lists '432 & 447 units' for this launch, but current real-estate listings describe a single built 447-unit development, and a secondary source notes a 2003-listed Spring Lodge phase (block 465) was cancelled while a different block (466) was actually built; the confirmed-built total (447) is used here, with the housingmap 432 figure treated as possibly referring to the cancelled/unbuilt phase rather than being fabricated. No pricing recoverable for Spring Lodge.
