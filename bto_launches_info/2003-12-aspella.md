## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, all 4-Room Premium. housingmap.sg's roadmap lists this as two entries ('Aspella 1' and 'Aspella 2', 429 & 433 units), but every secondary source found describes one development ('eight blocks of 862 four-room flats') with no mention of two distinct launches — 429+433=862 reconciles exactly, so recorded as a single launch/project here (862 units), same treatment as Mar 2004's Atrina. Notable: Aspella was positioned as a Premium contract with higher-end finishes (timber-strip flooring, bay windows, vanity tops), priced 15-20% above standard BTO flats of the era, and is cited in later retrospectives as one of the most profitable BTO projects ever on a percentage basis — reportedly outperforming even Pinnacle@Duxton in percentage terms. No pricing or exact application dates recoverable from any source found. application_start recorded as 2003-12-01 (first of the housingmap-designated month) purely so status computes 'closed'.
