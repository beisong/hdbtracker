## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, all 4-Room. **housingmap.sg's roadmap lists this as two entries, 'Atrina 1' and 'Atrina 2' (424 & 366 units, summing to 790)**, but a contemporaneous Straits Times article (25 Mar 2004) describes a single press event for 'Atrina' (HDB's 'third build-to-order project offered near Buangkok MRT station in the past six months', receiving 551 applications) with no mention of two distinct phases, and current real-estate listings describe one merged 'Atrina' development of 760 units across 8 blocks (272A-D, 273A-D), all 4-Room. Recorded as a single launch/project here (760 units) rather than splitting into two, since the primary press coverage and the built development both point to one exercise — housingmap's 'Atrina 1 & 2' split and its 790 combined total are treated as likely artifacts of how their table represents this development, not evidence of two genuinely separate BTO exercises. No pricing recoverable from any source found. application_start recorded as 2004-03-25 (the confirmed newspaper article date, very likely at or near the actual launch day) — application_end not found, left null.
