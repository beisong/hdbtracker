## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project — **Pinnacle@Duxton, Singapore's most famous public housing landmark** (seven connected 50-storey towers, the world's tallest public housing at completion, multiple international architecture awards, two 500m sky bridges). Unlike the surrounding 2004-2005 Sengkang/Punggol launches, this one is extensively documented: launched 29 May 2004 with an initial tranche of 528 units (of 7 towers); overwhelming demand (3,149 applications within one day) led HDB to release the remaining 1,320 units and extend the application window rather than proceed with a phased rollout — final total 1,848 units, 5,171 applications received (confirmed via multiple independent sources). Two flat types: S1 (≈ 4-Room equivalent, 93-97 sqm, 1,232 units, $289,200-$380,900) and S2 (≈ 5-Room equivalent, 105-108 sqm, 616 units, $345,100-$439,400) — 1,232+616=1,848 reconciles exactly, and the price figures are corroborated across multiple independent sources. 35 different unit layout variations were offered (balcony/planter/bay-window combinations) but priced within these two published ranges. Application close date not found precisely (one source notes 'by July 2004 the launch had closed') — recorded null; launch date is firmly confirmed so status still computes 'closed'. Project name 'PINNACLE @ DUXTON' used per this dataset's established @ -spacing convention (OneMap's own SEARCHVAL for this exact spelling matches; a separate 'THE PINNACLE@DUXTON' variant also exists in OneMap but isn't used here for consistency with the rest of the dataset).
