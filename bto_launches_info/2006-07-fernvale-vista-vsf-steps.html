Apply under this Build-To-Order and take the first step towards owning a desirable apartment at Fernvale Vista.
STEP 1: APPLY NOW
  • Submit your application1 through the following channels:
    :: Electronically through e-Sales > Build-To-Order Jul 06 > Apply Online;
     :: By hand at the Sales Office Counters (Atrium, 1st Storey, HDB Hub) Monday to Friday from 8:00am to 5:00pm and Saturday from 8:00am to 1:00pm or by depositing it in the drop-box provided at the Sales Office (near the Drop-Off Point), or Habitat Forum (BizFour, 3rd Storey, HDB Hub); or
    :: By post using the self-addressed envelope.
  • Indicate your choice for 2-room, 3-room or 4-room flat2.
  • Request for priority under the various priority schemes such as Third Child Priority, Tenants’ Priority Scheme and Married Child Priority Scheme, where applicable.
  • Pay an administrative fee of $10 for electronic application and $20 for application by hand/post.
  • Apply by 14th August 2006.

1 You may check on the latest number of applications received at e-Sales > Build-To-Order Jul 06 > Supply & Demand.
2 Different eligibility conditions apply for 2-room, 3-room and 4-room flats.
Please refer to Buying a new flat (e-Sales) > Policies > Eligibility Conditions to buy a flat from HDB.

 
STEP 2: SELECT YOUR FLAT 3
  • Applicants with queue positions of up to two times the flat supply, determined by computer ballot, will be invited to select a flat.
  • Invited applicants will be given an appointment to select a flat.
  • Select your choice flat in October 2006.
  • Pay an option fee of $500 for a 2-room flat, $1,000 for a 3-room flat or $2,000 for a 4-room flat by cash/NETS/cheque.

3 HDB will assess the applications received before deciding whether to proceed with the inviting of applicants for the booking of the apartments at Fernvale Vista.

 
STEP 3: SIGN AGREEMENT FOR LEASE 4
  • Sign the Agreement for Lease within 4 months from the date of selection of the flat.
  • Pay 10%5 of the selling price as downpayment and about 1% to 3% as legal and stamp fees by CPF and/or cash.

4 HDB will proceed to call tender to build Fernvale Vista only if majority of the flats are booked.
5 (a) First-timer applicants who qualify for the Staggered Downpayment Scheme need only pay a 5% downpayment. The balance 5% is payable at the time of collecting keys to the flat.
(b) Applicants taking a loan from a bank/financial institution have to pay 5% in cash and the balance 5% by CPF and/or cash.
 
STEP 4: COLLECT KEYS TO YOUR FLAT
  • HDB will invite you to collect the keys to your new flat when completed.6
  • Arrange to pay the balance of the selling price by an HDB loan (if eligible and subject to credit assessment and full utilization of the balance in CPF Ordinary Account) or a loan from the bank/financial institution, or by cash/CPF.

6 The Estimated Delivery Possession Date (EDPD) is 31st Jan 2012, an estimate of the date that HDB is required under the Agreement for Lease to deliver possession of the flat. Applicants will be informed of the actual Delivery Possession Date during the signing of Agreement for Lease. The flats may be completed earlier.
