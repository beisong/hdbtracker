## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, all 4-Room Standard flats (contract Geylang N5 C27), a mature-town BTO site in Geylang — secondary sources indicate this site was primarily built for SERS (Selective En-bloc Redevelopment Scheme) affected residents, with remaining flats re-offered for sale via BTO. Recovered from HDB's legacy VSF microsite (www.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu10p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback) as plain-text HTML: exact official table gives 447 units, 90-92 sqm internal, $230,000-$292,000, matching housingmap.sg's total exactly. Application close date (15 Nov 2006) confirmed from the VSF's own steps page. Launch/application-start day not found in any source — recorded as 2006-10-25, an APPROXIMATION consistent with housingmap.sg's 'October 2006' month designation and a ~3-week window before the confirmed 15 Nov close (a longer-than-2-week window, consistent with several other pre-2008 launches in this backfill). Same caveat as other approximated dates: application_start is used only for internal status computation, never displayed to users for a closed launch.
