## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, Standard flats (contract Sengkang N4C8). A later, distinct phase from Fernvale Vista 1 (Jul 2006, 508 units, not yet added to this dataset) — uses project key 'FERNVALE VISTA (PHASE 2)' to stay distinct once Phase 1 is added, following the same disambiguation convention as Jade Spring @ Yishun's two phases. Recovered from HDB's legacy VSF microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu05p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback, with an earlier capture also seen on a www69.hdb.gov.sg mirror) as plain-text HTML: exact official table gives 164x2-Room (45 sqm internal, $60,000-$83,000), 174x3-Room (65 sqm internal, $100,000-$139,000), 340x4-Room (90-91 sqm internal, $145,000-$200,000), reconciling exactly to 678 units, matching housingmap.sg's total exactly. Application close date (13 Jun 2007) confirmed directly from the VSF's own steps page; application_start (30 May 2007) derived from the exact 14-day BTO window pattern AND independently corroborated by the earliest Wayback capture of this project's VSF microsite being dated 30 May 2007 (proving the site was already live that day) — higher confidence than the pure-inference approximations used for Aug/Nov 2007.
