# Coral Spring

**Launch:** September 2007 BTO (2007-09-20 to 2007-10-09)
**Town:** SENGKANG (Non-Mature Town)
**Location:** Bounded by Sengkang West Avenue and Fernvale Road, near Fernvale LRT station. Premium flats.

## Flat Supply

| Type | Floor Area (sqm) | Units | Price Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4-Room | 92 | 698 | $188,000 - $252,000 |

## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, all 4-Room Premium flats (contract SK N4 C3). Recovered from HDB's legacy VSF microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu09p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback) as plain-text HTML: exact official table gives 698 units, 92 sqm internal, $188,000-$252,000 — matching housingmap.sg's total exactly. Application period (20 Sep - 9 Oct 2007) confirmed both from the VSF's own steps page ('Apply by 9 October 2007') and independently from a contemporaneous Straits Times article (reproduced on a property blog, dated 20 Sep 2007, describing the exhibition/application period as running 'from Thursday till Oct 9') — the two sources agree exactly.
