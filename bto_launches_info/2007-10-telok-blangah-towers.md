## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Two projects launched together (25 Oct 2007 - 14 Nov 2007, exact dates confirmed directly from HDB's own press release, reproduced verbatim on a property blog). Telok Blangah Towers (Bukit Merah, Premium contract, notable as a rare in-town/mature-estate BTO site near the CBD and VivoCity) and Punggol Lodge (Punggol, Standard contract) both have complete official-press-release-derived data: Telok Blangah Towers 90x Studio Apartment ($70,000-$91,000) + 100x3-Room ($187,000-$238,000) + 210x4-Room ($308,000-$402,000), reconciling exactly to 400 units; Punggol Lodge 52x3-Room ($122,000-$150,000) + 464x4-Room ($190,000-$234,000), reconciling exactly to 516 units. Both totals also reconcile to the press release's own combined headline (916 flats). Recovered via a contemporaneous Straits Times article (reproduced on the same property blog) that quotes the complete official price table verbatim, including the Studio Apartment and 3-Room figures the initial press-release-reproduction blog post omitted. Floor area not published in any source found for either project — recorded null. Studio Apartment units have no resale-market equivalent so resale_flat_type is null for that row, consistent with the convention used elsewhere in this dataset.
