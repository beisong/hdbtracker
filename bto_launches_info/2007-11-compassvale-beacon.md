## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Two projects launched together in November 2007 (exact day not recoverable from any source found — application_start recorded as 2007-11-01, an APPROXIMATION flagging month-only precision, not a confirmed launch day; this is only used internally for status computation, the frontend never displays application_start for a closed launch, only application_end, which is also unrecoverable and left null). Compassvale Beacon's exact official price table was recovered from its legacy VSF microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu11p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback): 60x2-Room (45 sqm internal, $69,000-$87,000) + 150x3-Room (65 sqm internal, $116,000-$143,000) + 540x4-Room (90-92 sqm internal, $180,000-$233,000), reconciling exactly to the 750-unit combined headline (Standard flats, contract SK N2 C3B). Segar Meadows' own VSF price page was never crawled by Wayback (only the outer about/map/model frame pages exist) — genuinely unrecoverable at the per-type level. **Unit-total conflict**: housingmap.sg's roadmap lists Segar Meadows as 412 units, but multiple independent real-estate aggregators (srx.com.sg, stackproperty.sg) consistently report 712 units (3-Room and 4-Room only, no 2-Room) — treated 712 as correct here (multiple independent corroborating sources vs. housingmap's single figure, and this is the second housingmap total discrepancy found in this backfill after Mar 2008's Jade Spring 572-vs-576 case). No per-type split or pricing found anywhere — recorded as a single unrecoverable-split placeholder row (resale_flat_type null) per the established convention.
