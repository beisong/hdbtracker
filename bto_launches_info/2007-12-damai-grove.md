## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Two projects launched together (27 Dec 2007 per a contemporaneous Business Times article dated 28 Dec 2007, reproduced on a property blog, describing the launch as 'yesterday'). This is the earliest launch this backfill has reached where HDB's legacy VSF microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu12p.nsf/0/07DECBTOPG_page, 07DECBTOYI_page) was found in Wayback's CDX index but only its outer frame page was ever actually crawled — no price/steps/flats text pages exist for either project, a genuine archive gap distinct from the several transient 'Internet Archive Temporarily Offline' 503s seen elsewhere in this backfill (which always recovered on retry). The Business Times article gives combined totals (1,122 flats: 110x3-Room + 1,012x4-Room) plus each project's exact 4-Room unit count and indicative price range: Damai Grove 676x4-Room ($195,000-$240,000), Jade Spring @ Yishun (Phase 1) 336x4-Room ($183,000-$246,000). Cross-checking against housingmap.sg's per-project totals (Damai Grove 738, Jade Spring 1 384) lets the 3-Room split be derived precisely: Damai Grove 738-676=62x3-Room, Jade Spring 1 384-336=48x3-Room (62+48=110, matching the article's combined 3-Room count exactly) — but no 3-Room price was published in any source found, so those two rows use the partial-data pattern (units confirmed, price/floor-area null). Floor area not published for any row in this launch. Application close date not recoverable from any source — recorded null (launch date 27 Dec 2007 is confirmed, so status still computes 'closed').
