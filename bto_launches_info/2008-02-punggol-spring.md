## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, all 4-Room Standard flats — HDB's own 'first BTO development for 2008'. A Business Times article reproduced on a property blog (article dated 27 Feb 2008, describing the launch as 'yesterday') confirms 494 units, all 4-Room, priced $204,000-$259,000. Floor area (90-94 sqm internal, two unit variants) confirmed from the project's legacy VSF microsite 'flats' page (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu02p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback) — recorded as 90 sqm (lower bound). The VSF's own price/steps pages were never crawled by Wayback (repeated fetch attempts returned the site's generic 'not found' page despite CDX listing a 200 status, unlike every other genuine 503 'Internet Archive Temporarily Offline' hit elsewhere in this backfill, which recovered on retry) — application close date not recoverable from any source found, recorded null (launch date 26 Feb 2008 is confirmed, so `launchStatus()` still correctly reports 'closed').
