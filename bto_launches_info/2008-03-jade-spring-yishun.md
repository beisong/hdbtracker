# Jade Spring @ Yishun (Phase 2)

**Launch:** March 2008 BTO (2008-03-18 to 2008-03-31)
**Town:** YISHUN (Non-Mature Town)
**Location:** Junction of Yishun Ring Road and Yishun Avenue 11, near Yishun town centre and MRT station.

## Flat Supply

| Type | Floor Area (sqm) | Units | Price Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2-Room | 45 | 36 | $77,000 - $97,000 |
| 3-Room | 65 | 72 | $124,000 - $141,000 |
| 4-Room | 90 | 468 | $189,000 - $253,000 |

## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, Standard flats (contract Yishun N4 C6). Recovered from HDB's legacy 'virtual showflat' microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu03p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback) as plain-text HTML. Exact official price table gives 36x2-Room (45 sqm internal, $77,000-$97,000), 72x3-Room (65 sqm internal, $124,000-$141,000), 468x4-Room (90-94 sqm internal, $189,000-$253,000), reconciling exactly to 576 units — note housingmap.sg's roadmap lists this launch's total as 572 units, off by 4 from the verified official figure; a contemporaneous property blog post independently titled 'HDB launches 576-unit Yishun project' and quoting the identical per-type prices confirms 576 is correct, so housingmap.sg's total was treated as the error here, not the official source. Application period (18-31 Mar 2008) corroborated by two independent contemporaneous news sources.
