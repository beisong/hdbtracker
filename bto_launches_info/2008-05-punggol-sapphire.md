## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Two projects launched together (22 May 2008, applications close 4 Jun 2008 — dates confirmed via a contemporaneous Straits Times article reproduced on a property blog, dated 23 May 2008 and referring to 'Yesterday's launches'). Compassvale Pearl's exact official price table was recovered from its legacy VSF microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu05p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback): 336x4-Room (90-92 sqm internal, $216,000-$269,000) + 84x5-Room (110-113 sqm internal, $308,000-$367,000), reconciling exactly to 420 units — the Straits Times article's quoted price bookends ($216,000 to $367,000) match this table exactly, validating the article as a reliable source. Punggol Sapphire's own VSF price table page was never crawled by Wayback (only a 404'd capture exists), and no www100.hdb.gov.sg brochure PDF was found either — genuinely unrecoverable at the per-type detail level. Punggol Sapphire's unit split (760x4-Room + 282x5-Room Premium + 23x5-Room Loft, reconciling exactly to the 1,065-unit combined headline) and price bookends (4-Room from $234,000; 5-Room Loft up to $477,000) are from the same Straits Times article, cross-validated as reliable via the Compassvale Pearl match above, but no per-type floor area, 4-Room max, 5-Room Premium range, or 5-Room Loft min could be found in any source — recorded null where genuinely unconfirmed (partial-data pattern) rather than guessed. The 5-Room Loft type has no direct resale-market equivalent (higher ceiling, mezzanine-style unit) so resale_flat_type is null for that row.
