# Fernvale Residence

**Launch:** June 2008 BTO (Punggol Breeze / Fernvale Residence) (2008-06-30 to 2008-07-14)
**Town:** SENGKANG (Non-Mature Town)
**Location:** Fernvale Road, Sengkang. Premium flats, 5 blocks 20-25 storeys.

## Flat Supply

| Type | Floor Area (sqm) | Units | Price Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3-Room | 65 | 55 | $133,000 - $162,000 |
| 4-Room | 90 | 444 | $207,000 - $275,000 |
| 5-Room | 110 | 124 | $293,000 - $365,000 |

## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Two standalone projects launched together (30 Jun 2008, applications close 14 Jul 2008 per contemporaneous blog coverage) — a separate, later BTO exercise from Straits Vista @ Marsiling (2008-06a, applications closed 23 Jun 2008). Recovered from each project's own legacy VSF microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu06p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback) as plain-text HTML: Punggol Breeze (Premium flats, contract Punggol East C33) 778x4-Room (90 sqm internal, $223,000-$278,000) + 186x5-Room (110-115 sqm internal, $315,000-$382,000), reconciling exactly to 964 units; Fernvale Residence (Premium flats, contract Sengkang N4 C16) 55x3-Room (65 sqm internal, $133,000-$162,000) + 444x4-Room (90-91 sqm internal, $207,000-$275,000) + 124x5-Room (110 sqm internal, $293,000-$365,000), reconciling exactly to 623 units.
