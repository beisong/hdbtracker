At 15 to 17 storeys high, the twelve blocks at Punggol Breeze offer a splendid collection of 4-room and 5-room Premium flats. These blocks are lined strategically along the periphery of the development so that many units come with views of the internal lush landscaping.

The two-storey carpark comes with a roof garden, adding to the soothing greenery of the precinct. With direct lift access to the carpark from every block, accessing your home is convenient and hassle-free whether rain or shine.

Each unit comes with full height windows in the living room and three-quarter height windows in the bedrooms. Some units are provided with bay windows in the master bedroom. Balconies are also provided in some units.
 

These Premium flats are tastefully adorned with glazed porcelain ceramic floor tiles for the living and dining areas, timber strip flooring for the bedrooms, and ceramic wall/floor tiles for the kitchen and bathrooms. Quality sanitary fittings in the bathrooms add the final touches to your contemporary home.
