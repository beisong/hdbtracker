## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, Standard flats (contract Bukit Panjang N6 C9). Recovered from HDB's legacy 'virtual showflat' microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu08p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback) as plain-text HTML — the brochure PDF at www100.hdb.gov.sg for this launch 404s, but the VSF microsite survived. Exact official price table gives 96x2-Room (45 sqm internal, $82,000-$106,000), 94x3-Room (65 sqm internal, $138,000-$170,000), 284x4-Room (90 sqm internal, $211,000-$270,000), reconciling exactly to the 474-unit combined headline. Application close date (8 Sep 2008) confirmed directly from the site's own steps page.
