# Punggol Arcadia

**Launch:** November 2008 BTO (2008-11-13 to 2008-11-26)
**Town:** PUNGGOL (Non-Mature Town)
**Location:** Junction of Punggol Place and Punggol Field, near Punggol MRT/LRT station and bus interchange. Premium flats.

## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, Premium flats. Recovered from HDB's legacy www100.hdb.gov.sg brochure host via Wayback (same CDX sweep that surfaced March 2009's Nautilus brochure). Official Indicative Price Range table gives 120x3-Room (68-69 sqm internal, $181,000-$211,000), 465x4-Room (94-95 sqm internal, $268,000-$327,000), 165x5-Room (110-111 sqm internal, $356,000-$416,000), reconciling exactly to the 750-unit combined headline. Application close date (26 Nov 2008) confirmed directly from the brochure's own 'Simple Steps to Apply' page. Floor area recorded using the lower bound of the internal range.
