Bask in the convenience of modern comforts.

Located at the junction of Yishun Ring Road and Yishun Street 41, Dew Spring @ Yishun is only a short walk from the neigbourhood centre which hosts a wide range of essential services and retail trades that will serve your daily needs.

Home is also well-connected to the city by major roads, expressways and bus routes. The MRT just minutes away, ensures easy commuting island-wide.

Parents with young children will appreciate the childcare centre conveniently located within the development.

For loads of shopping, dining and entertainment choices, head to the nearby Northpoint Shopping Centre which is currently being upgraded to serve you better. Check out its brand new extension which boasts a newly opened public library and a broader shop mix.

With upcoming plans to transform the town centre into a vibrant hub with improved pedestrian mall, a bazaar place, a new shopping complex integrated with housing and bus interchange, Yishun is set to be even more pulsating in time to come.

Come 2010, Yishun welcomes the first hospital in the North – the Khoo Teck Puat Hospital which overlooks the scenic Yishun Pond. Spanning over 3.5 hectares, the 550-bed hospital will offer a full spectrum of healthcare services when it is fully opened.
