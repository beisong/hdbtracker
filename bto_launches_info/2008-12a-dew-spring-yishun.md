# Dew Spring @ Yishun

**Launch:** December 2008 BTO (Dew Spring) (2008-12-18 to 2008-12-31)
**Town:** YISHUN (Non-Mature Town)
**Location:** Junction of Yishun Ring Road and Yishun Street 41, near Northpoint Shopping Centre.

| Type | Floor Area (sqm) | Units | Price Range |

| 2-Room | 45 | 144 | $76,000 - $90,000 |

| 4-Room | 90 | 504 | $197,000 - $238,000 |

## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, Standard flats. A separate, earlier BTO exercise from the 30 Dec 2008 Sunshine Court/Punggol Regalia launch (2008-12) despite both falling in December 2008 — confirmed distinct because the Sunshine Court/Punggol Regalia official press release does not mention Dew Spring at all, and this project's own application period (18-31 Dec 2008) predates and does not overlap that launch's (30 Dec 2008 - 12 Jan 2009). Recovered from HDB's legacy 'virtual showflat' microsite (www101.hdb.gov.sg/hdbvsf/eampu12p.nsf/..., captured in Wayback) as plain-text HTML: exact official price table gives 144x2-Room (45 sqm internal, $76,000-$90,000), 216x3-Room (65 sqm internal, $120,000-$146,000), 504x4-Room (90 sqm internal, $197,000-$238,000), reconciling exactly to the 864-unit combined headline. Application close date (31 Dec 2008) confirmed directly from the site's own steps page; launch/start date (18 Dec 2008) per housingmap.sg's roadmap, not independently re-verified beyond that.
