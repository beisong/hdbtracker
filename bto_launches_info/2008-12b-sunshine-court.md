# Sunshine Court

**Launch:** December 2008 BTO (Sunshine Court / Punggol Regalia) (2008-12-30 to 2009-01-12)
**Town:** CHOA CHU KANG (Non-Mature Town)
**Location:** Choa Chu Kang Avenue 5, opposite Sunshine Place. Standard flats plus elderly-only Studio Apartments (first offered in Choa Chu Kang).

## Flat Supply

| Type | Floor Area (sqm) | Units | Price Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studio Apartment (35 sqm) | 35 | 60 | $58,000 - $66,000 |
| Studio Apartment (45 sqm) | 45 | 104 | $72,000 - $80,000 |
| 3-Room | 65 | 117 | $121,000 - $148,000 |
| 4-Room | 90 | 171 | $202,000 - $236,000 |

## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Two standalone projects launched together under the same BTO exercise: Sunshine Court (Standard contract) and Punggol Regalia (Premium contract) — note this is a DIFFERENT December 2008 exercise from Dew Spring @ Yishun (18 Dec 2008), which HDB's own press release for this exercise does not mention, confirming Dew Spring was a separate contract recorded as its own launch entry. Official press release recovered directly from the National Archives of Singapore as plain HTML (not PDF) — the most complete official source type found in this backfill, giving exact per-project unit counts and Annex 1 pricing/floor-area tables verbatim: Sunshine Court 164 Studio Apartments (60x35sqm + 104x45sqm, cross-verified against its www100.hdb.gov.sg brochure) + 117x3-Room (65sqm internal, $121,000-$148,000) + 171x4-Room (90sqm internal, $202,000-$236,000), reconciling exactly to 452 units; Punggol Regalia 546x4-Room (90-93sqm internal, $252,000-$316,000) + 183x5-Room (110-114sqm internal, $342,000-$428,000), reconciling exactly to 729 units. Application period (30 Dec 2008 to 12 Jan 2009) confirmed directly from the press release's own text.
