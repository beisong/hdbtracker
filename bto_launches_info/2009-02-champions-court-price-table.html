Champions Court offers an alluring mix of Studio Apartments, 3-room, 4-room and 5-room Standard flats.

These functional Studio Apartments are designed specially to meet the needs of those 55 years and above. At a compact size of 35 sqm or 45 sqm, each apartment will be in ready-to-move-in condition and fully furnished with essentials such as elderly-friendly fixtures, ceramic floor tiles, wall tiles, built-in wardrobe, kitchen cabinets and more.

The 3-room, 4-room and 5-room Standard flats are provided with quality floor finishes in the kitchen, household shelter, service yard and bathrooms. For hassle-free renovation, opt in for the Optional Component Scheme and have full floor finishes and internal doors installed in your flat. Cost will be added to the selling price of the flat.

All units come with three-quarter height windows in the living/dining areas and main bedroom.
