**Location:** Punggol Walk. Premium Flats project. No 2-Room or 3-Room flats in this project.

## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Single standalone project, Premium Flats. Upgraded with exact official data recovered from HDB's own legacy brochure host (www100.hdb.gov.sg, captured in Wayback back to 2008) after this backfill discovered that archive — supersedes an earlier version with null floor areas sourced only from a RenoTalk forum thread and a secondary blog. The brochure's official Indicative Price Range table gives 615×4-Room (92-97 sqm / 91-96 sqm internal, $264,000-$322,000) and 154×5-Room (116 sqm / 114 sqm internal, $344,000-$409,000), reconciling exactly to the 769-unit combined headline and matching the previously-recovered unit counts and prices exactly. Floor area recorded using the lower bound of the internal range.
