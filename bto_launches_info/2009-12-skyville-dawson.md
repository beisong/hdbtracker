## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. This is the historically notable launch of **SkyVille @ Dawson and SkyTerrace @ Dawson**, HDB's landmark high-rise 'housing in the park' Dawson estate redevelopment under the Remaking Our Heartland programme — 1,718 combined units, almost 12x oversubscribed at launch. Application start date (15 Dec 2009) confirmed via NLB's Singapore History eresources database. **Exact per-block unit-distribution tables recovered from housingmap.sg's 'Maps & Plans' brochure PDFs** (page with 'Block Number / 2 Room / 3 Room / 4 Room / Total' grid) for all four projects — supersedes an earlier null-placeholder version of this launch that couldn't find a per-type split (the official HDB press release itself was never located via Wayback). Floor areas confirmed from each project's 'General Info' brochure floor-plan pages. Only 'from' prices are available (no price ceiling published anywhere found) — recorded as price_min with price_max null. SkyTerrace @ Dawson has an unusually rich unit-type mix: besides standalone Studio Apartment/3-Room/4-Room/5-Room, it has 4-Room and 5-Room 'Loft' duplex variants each paired with a smaller 44 sqm Studio Apartment unit on a different floor (65 such paired Studio units total, on top of 40 standalone 37 sqm ones) — all summed from the brochure's own detailed table (40+39+26 Studio Apartment rows = 105; matches distinct SA count). SkyVille @ Dawson and Segar Grove and Montreal Dale have no loft/paired-unit complexity.
