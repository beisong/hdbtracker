## Curation Note

**Gap-fill**: this launch was missing from the dataset despite full brochure coverage being available — caught during a systematic month-by-month completeness re-check of housingmap.sg's entire roadmap against the dataset. Two projects launched together, application 16-29 Mar 2010 (confirmed via housingmap.sg's launch date plus an independent contemporaneous news article headlined 'HDB offers 828 new BTO flats', which matches the combined total exactly: 522+306=828). Fernvale Ridge (Sengkang, Standard flats) has a full official Indicative Price Range table from its housingmap.sg brochure: 180x3-Room (65 sqm internal, $128,000-$171,000), 216x4-Room (90 sqm internal, $216,000-$271,000), 126x5-Room (110-113 sqm internal, $281,000-$352,000), reconciling exactly to 522 units, matching housingmap.sg's total exactly. Sembawang RiverLodge (Sembawang, Standard flats) is a special case: the brochure states the physical development comprises 432 units total (2-, 3- and 4-Room), but explicitly says only 306 units (3-Room and 4-Room) were actually offered in this BTO exercise — the 126 2-Room units were built but deliberately withheld from public sale, reserved for lower-income families at a later date. Recorded here as 306 units (86x3-Room, 65 sqm internal, $128,000-$166,000; 220x4-Room, 90 sqm internal, $212,000-$268,000), matching what was actually launched/sold in this BTO exercise, not the full physical development — consistent with this dataset's established convention of recording the BTO offering, not the built total. housingmap.sg lists 432 for this project, which is the full development size, not the offered-for-sale amount; this is a deliberate, documented divergence, not an error.
