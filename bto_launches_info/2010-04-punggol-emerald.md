## Curation note

**Gap-fill**: caught during the same systematic housingmap.sg completeness re-check as 2010-03. Two projects launched together in Punggol, application 20 Apr - 3 May 2010 (confirmed via an archived HDB InfoWEB e-service page showing 'Number of Applications Received as at 3 May 2010', and a contemporaneous news article confirming the 3 May application close). Unlike most other launches, neither project's General_Info.pdf brochure (the usual source for the Indicative Price Range table) contains any pricing at all in this brochure format — it has only descriptive text, floor plans and general specifications. Unit counts (Punggol Emerald: 188 Studio Apartment / 96 3-Room / 350 4-Room / 222 5-Room, total 856; Punggol Waves: 105 3-Room / 259 4-Room / 209 5-Room, total 573) were instead sourced from each project's Maps&Plans.pdf 'Block Number' unit-distribution table, and independently cross-checked against the archived HDB e-service application-count page, which reports the combined totals for both projects together (Studio Apartment 188, 3-room 201 [=96+105], 4-room 609 [=350+259], 5-room 431 [=222+209], grand total 1429 [=856+573]) — an exact match on every figure. Only minimum indicative prices could be sourced (via aggregated secondary sources, not an official HDB price table) for each flat type: Punggol Emerald Studio Apartment from $73,000, 3-Room from $166,000, 4-Room from $262,000, 5-Room from $333,000; Punggol Waves 3-Room from $150,000, 4-Room from $243,000, 5-Room from $330,000. No maximum price could be recovered from any available source (official or secondary) despite an extensive search — recorded as price_max: null, consistent with this dataset's existing convention for min-only entries (e.g. 2012-05 PUNGGOL EDGE, 2011-04 ANCHORVALE COVE, 2009-12 SKYVILLE @ DAWSON). Punggol Emerald's Studio Apartment total of 188 units combines two sub-types (Type A ~35 sqm and Type B ~45 sqm internal, per the General_Info floor plans) whose individual unit counts are not broken out anywhere in the source material, so floor_area_sqm is left null for that row rather than guessed.
