## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Full press release (issued 28 Feb 2011) recovered from a secondary blog reproduction, giving exact unit counts for Fernvale Flora and Fernvale Gardens directly in prose, but the source's own copy is truncated right before Segar Vale's project-description paragraph (jumps straight to the pricing section). Segar Vale's exact breakdown (112×2-Room, 112×3-Room, 269×4-Room, 197×5-Room = 690 units) was reconstructed by subtracting Fernvale Flora + Fernvale Gardens' known per-type counts from the press release's own stated totals (205×2-Room, 219×3-Room, 800×4-Room, 369×5-Room = 1,593 overall) — the resulting 690-unit total independently matches btohq.com's published Segar Vale total exactly, confirming the reconstruction. Floor area (sqm) not published in this recovered source — recorded null, consistent with other early-2011 launches in this backfill.
