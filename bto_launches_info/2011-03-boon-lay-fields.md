## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Full press release recovered via Wayback Machine (old hdb.gov.sg fi10296p.nsf domain), giving exact per-project unit counts AND exact price ranges (min and max, not just tier-level 'from' prices) in prose — verified to sum exactly to the press release's own 1,527-unit headline (224 Studio Apartments, 233×3-Room, 666×4-Room, 404×5-Room). This launch introduced two policy changes: BTO application-to-selection turnaround halved (selection starting week 4 instead of week 9), and a Mobile@HDB iPhone app. The small Annex A/B PDFs (pricing/comparables detail, 27KB each) were never crawled by Wayback — so exact floor_area_sqm is not confirmed for this launch (recorded null, consistent with Apr 2011's partial-data pattern), while resale_flat_type/units/price_min/price_max are all exact and confirmed. Compassvale Ancilla's 224 Studio Apartment units are recorded as a single row (not split into two sizes) since the press release doesn't break down the SA count by size for this launch, only for larger BTO exercises with fuller tables.
