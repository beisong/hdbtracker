## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. This full press release (not just an Annex table) was recovered via kendata12345.wordpress.com and gives exact per-project unit counts in prose, verified to sum exactly to the press release's own stated breakdown (105×2-Room, 541×3-Room, 1,797×4-Room, 742×5-Room = 3,185 total) — this was 'the largest supply of BTO flats in a single launch since 2002' at the time. However, the actual Annex A/B (pricing + resale comparables, PDF 32KB/42KB) referenced by the press release was never crawled by Wayback (confirmed via CDX search on the hdb.gov.sg fi10297p.nsf domain) — so this launch has a genuine partial gap different from other unrecoverable-split cases: **unit counts and flat types are exact and confirmed**, but **floor_area_sqm and price_max are not published anywhere in the recovered source** (only tier-level 'from' prices: Standard tier — Hougang Parkview & Montreal Ville — $92,000/2-Room, $158,000/3-Room, $248,000/4-Room, $333,000/5-Room; Premium tier — Anchorvale Cove & Waterway Terraces II — $179,000/3-Room, $286,000/4-Room, $354,000/5-Room). Recorded with floor_area_sqm and price_max both null (frontend renders '-- sqft' and '$X–$--' gracefully, confirmed via formatNumber's null handling) while resale_flat_type and price_min are set normally, since those ARE confirmed — a different, more-complete partial-data pattern than the full null-placeholder rows used for Punggol Edge/Ping Yi Greens/Waterway Banks/Fajar Spring, where even the per-type split was unknown.
