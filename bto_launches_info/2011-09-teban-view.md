## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. **Official Annex A1 recovered in full** ('INFORMATION ON SEPTEMBER 2011 BUILD-TO-ORDER FLATS'), a genuine HDB press-release PDF archived by kendata12345.wordpress.com. Reconciles exactly to this launch's 5,415-unit BTO portion (part of a bumper joint BTO+SBF launch of 8,200+ flats total; the SBF portion, 2,847 units, is excluded per convention). Two project PAIRS share one combined pricing table in the official Table A1a, but the Annex's own prose paragraphs give exact independent unit counts per project (verified to sum exactly to each combined table row) — so, unlike other combined-entry cases in this backfill, these are recorded as FOUR standalone project entries (not two combined ones) to preserve accurate per-project locations/unit-counts for mapping and search: Anchorvale Harvest (717 units, no Studio Apartment) & Fernvale Rivergrove (1,728 units, has Studio Apartment) in Sengkang; Waterway Brooks (1,312 units, has Studio Apartment, no 3-Room) & Waterway Woodcress (694 units, has 3-Room, no Studio Apartment) in Punggol. Since only the COMBINED price range is published per shared flat type (not a per-project split), both projects in each pair share the same price_min/price_max and representative floor_area_sqm for their shared flat types — this is the one piece of data that remains blended, while every unit count is exact and independently sourced. Golden Peony (Jurong West, Studio Apartment only), Teban View (Jurong East), and Yio Chu Kang Vista (Ang Mo Kio) are fully standalone with no shared-table complications.
