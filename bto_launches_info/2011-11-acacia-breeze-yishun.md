## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Official Annex A (PDF 1478KB) could not be recovered via Wayback. Reconciles exactly to the press release's own 4,235-unit BTO headline (Bedok, Bukit Panjang, Hougang, Punggol, Yishun). Acacia Breeze @ Yishun's 5-Room price_min corrected to $277,000 (matching singpromos's independently-sourced summary) after the kendata image's OCR-rendered figure ($227,000) proved internally inconsistent. Hougang Capeview and Hougang DewCourt share one combined official pricing table but the underlying tender data comes from two genuinely separate building contracts — kept as two standalone entries. Waterway Banks and Fajar Spring — initially recorded with unrecoverable-split placeholders — were **upgraded with exact per-type unit counts, floor areas, and full price ranges** recovered from housingmap.sg's 'Maps & Plans' and 'General Info' brochure PDFs.
