## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Official Annex A (PDF 1478KB) could not be recovered via Wayback. Reconciles exactly to the press release's own 4,235-unit BTO headline (Bedok, Bukit Panjang, Hougang, Punggol, Yishun). Recovered full official-quality per-project tender-contract data (exact block/contract-level unit counts, not estimates) for 5 of 7 projects from kendata12345.wordpress.com's rasterized cost-analysis images. Hougang Capeview and Hougang DewCourt share one combined official pricing table (per singpromos's Table 1) but the underlying tender data comes from two genuinely separate building contracts with real independent unit counts per flat type — kept as two standalone entries rather than combined, since true per-project splits exist (unlike Punggol Edge/Ping Yi Greens below). Acacia Breeze @ Yishun's 5-Room price_min corrected to $277,000 (matching singpromos's independently-sourced summary) after the kendata image's OCR-rendered figure ($227,000) proved internally inconsistent (lower than the 4-Room minimum). **Third and fourth unrecoverable-split cases in this backfill**: Fajar Spring (264 units of Studio Apartment + 3-Room) and Waterway Banks (1,016 units of 2-/3-/4-Room, standard tier alongside premium-tier Waterway Ridges) both have confirmed totals (via reconciliation and independently via btohq.com) but no source publishes their per-type unit splits — recorded with the same null-placeholder pattern as Punggol Edge (May 2012) and Ping Yi Greens (Mar 2012).
