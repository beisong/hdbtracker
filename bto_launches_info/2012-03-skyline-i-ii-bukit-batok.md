## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. NOTE: the singpromos.com URL slug is mis-dated ('28 May – 3 Apr 2012') but the article's own body text and issue date (28 Mar 2012) confirm this is the March 2012 exercise, application period 28 Mar – 3 Apr 2012. Official Annex A referenced by the press release had a separate per-project PDF for each project rather than one combined file, none of which could be recovered via Wayback. singpromos.com had no unit-count table at all for this launch (only the price summary) — recovered full official-quality data for 6 of 7 project clusters from rasterized tender-cost-analysis images on kendata12345.wordpress.com. Reconciles exactly to the press release's own 4,153-unit BTO headline (2 non-mature towns: Bukit Batok, Bukit Panjang; 5 mature: Bukit Timah, Clementi, Geylang, Toa Payoh, Bedok). A concurrent SBF exercise offered another 3,825 balance flats across 26 towns — excluded per convention. Skyline I @ Bukit Batok & Skyline II @ Bukit Batok share one combined flat-supply table in both HDB's own official summary and kendata's cost-analysis image — kept as one combined entry, consistent with that shared official table (not a secondary-source artifact this time). **Ping Yi Greens is the second genuine unrecoverable-split case in this backfill** (after Punggol Edge, May 2012): it was HDB's first-ever Multi-Generation Priority Scheme (MGPS) pilot project, offering 2-/3-/4-Room flats (parents apply for 2-Room, married children for 2-Room-or-bigger, in the same project) — its 418-unit total is confirmed by both the reconciliation gap and btohq.com independently, and its per-type 'from' prices are known (2-Room $174,000 / 3-Room $281,000 / 4-Room $400,000), but no source publishes the exact unit split despite exhaustive search. Recorded with resale_flat_type/floor_area_sqm/price_min/price_max all null, same placeholder pattern as Punggol Edge.
