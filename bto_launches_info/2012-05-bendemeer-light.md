## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Official Annex A (PDF 5404KB) was linked in the Wayback-archived press-release page but the PDF itself was never crawled (same gap pattern as Jul 2012). singpromos.com's article had no detailed per-project unit-count table at all this time (only the price summary) — recovered full official-quality data for 5 of 6 projects from rasterized tender-cost-analysis images on kendata12345.wordpress.com (archived Annex-derived data, not fabricated). Reconciles exactly to the press release's own 4,627-unit BTO headline (2 non-mature towns: Choa Chu Kang, Punggol; 1 non-mature-adjacent Sengkang; 1 mature: Kallang/Whampoa). **Punggol Edge is a genuine, documented data gap**: its total (688 units, an integrated BTO+rental development — 688 BTO flats in 5 blocks plus a separate 446-unit rental block) is confirmed by both the reconciliation gap (4,627 minus the other 5 projects' known total of 3,939) and independently by btohq.com's project total, and its per-type 'from' prices are known (3-Room $163,000 / 4-Room $260,000 / 5-Room $326,000) from HDB's own Table 1 — but despite exhaustive search (Wayback CDX, kendata12345's image gallery which covers every OTHER project in this launch, btohq.com's project-spec page, multiple web/forum searches), no source publishes Punggol Edge's exact 3-Room/4-Room/5-Room unit split or price ceilings. Recorded as a single row with resale_flat_type/floor_area_sqm/price_min/price_max all null (schema-supported placeholder pattern, same as an unpriced upcoming project) rather than fabricating a split — units=688 is the one figure that IS certain. This is the first and only case in the entire historical backfill where a project's total is confirmed but its flat-type composition genuinely could not be recovered.
