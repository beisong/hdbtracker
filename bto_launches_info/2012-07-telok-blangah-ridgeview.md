## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Official Annex A (PDF 2356KB) referenced by the press release was linked in a Wayback-archived press-release page, but the PDF itself was never crawled by Wayback (confirmed via broad CDX search on the old hdb.gov.sg fi10297p.nsf domain — same domain where Nov 2012's Annex A WAS recoverable, but this one wasn't archived). Reconstructed primarily from singpromos.com's detailed per-project table, reconciling exactly to the press release's own 4,191-unit BTO headline (2 non-mature towns: Choa Chu Kang, Punggol; 3 mature towns: Bukit Merah, Clementi, Geylang). Clementi Gateway's singpromos table only showed a 'Typical Selling Price' summary with no per-type unit counts (same gap pattern as Keat Hong Mirage, Nov 2012) — recovered its exact 134 units of 3-Room / 144 units of 4-Room split (278 total, matching the reconciliation gap exactly) from a rasterized official cost-analysis table image on kendata12345.wordpress.com, which itself appears sourced from the real Annex A (its price ranges match singpromos's 'From' prices exactly). GreenTops @ Sims Place's Studio Apartment floor areas are 36/46 sqm rather than the usual 37/47 sqm seen elsewhere — verified directly in the source table, not a typo. All 7 projects are fully standalone — no combined entries needed.
