## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme. Official Annex could not be located via any channel (live URL, Wayback CDX, NAS) — reconstructed from singpromos.com's detailed per-project supply/price table, reconciling exactly to the press release's own 3,346-unit BTO headline (3 non-mature towns: Choa Chu Kang, Hougang, Yishun; 3 mature towns: Ang Mo Kio, Kallang/Whampoa, Tampines). First tranche of HDB's planned 23,000 BTO flats for 2013. This launch introduced the Parenthood Priority Scheme (PPS), announced 2013-01-21, setting aside 30%/50% of BTO/SBF supply for first-timer married couples with a young citizen child. All 6 projects are standalone — no combined entries needed. Whampoa Dew has two Studio Apartment size variants, kept as separate flat rows. No 2-Room flats offered in this launch at all.
