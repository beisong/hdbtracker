## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme — flats are plain '2-room' rather than '2-room Flexi'. Official Annex A1 (PDF 1747KB) referenced by the press release could not be located via any channel (live URL, Wayback CDX, NAS) — reconstructed from singpromos.com's detailed per-project supply/price table, reconciling exactly to the press release's own 4,900-unit BTO headline (8 projects, 5 non-mature towns: Choa Chu Kang, Hougang, Jurong West, Sembawang, Woodlands). This exercise was combined with a Sale of Balance Flats (SOBF) offering — SBF units excluded per convention. Launched three new first-timer/second-timer/elderly housing measures from the 2013 Committee of Supply debate (PPS extended to expectant mothers, doubled second-timer 2-/3-Room quota, 50% SA quota reserved via SAPS). 'EastBrook, EastWave & EastBank @ Canberra' is a 3-way partial-combine (first seen this granular in the backfill): 2-Room and 3-Room are exclusive to EastBrook; 4-Room is shared across all three (EastBrook, EastWave, EastBank); 5-Room is shared between EastWave and EastBank only (excluding EastBrook) — no further per-project split published for either shared row. 'Woodlands Pasture I & II' is a standard two-phase combine (phases sharing one flat-supply/pricing table). Golden Mint (Buangkok, Hougang town), Hougang Crimson, Keat Hong Crest (Choa Chu Kang), and Spring Haven @ Jurong (Jurong West) are all standalone.
