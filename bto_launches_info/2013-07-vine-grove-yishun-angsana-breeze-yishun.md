## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme — flats are plain '2-room' rather than '2-room Flexi'. Official Annex could not be located via any channel (live URL, Wayback CDX, NAS) — reconstructed from singpromos.com's detailed per-project supply/price table, reconciling exactly to the press release's own 3,861-unit headline (Bukit Merah, Sengkang, Yishun). This was the launch that introduced HDB's first-ever direct BTO purchase pathway for singles (previously resale-only) — a separate table of 301 new-project + 218 previous-exercise 2-Room 'additional'/balance flats for singles (519 total, matching the press release's own stated breakdown) was excluded per the established balance-flat convention. Vine Grove @ Yishun & Angsana Breeze @ Yishun combined per the same partial-combine pattern as Boon Lay View & Yung Kuang Court (Nov 2013): Vine Grove's two 2-Room rows and Angsana Breeze's 5-Room row are each exclusive to their own project, while 3-Room and 4-Room are jointly labeled across both with no further split published. Fernvale Riverwalk (Sengkang) and Telok Blangah ParcView (Bukit Merah, the launch's sole Mature Estate project) are both standalone.
