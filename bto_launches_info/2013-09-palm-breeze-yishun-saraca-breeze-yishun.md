## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme — flats are plain '2-room' rather than '2-room Flexi'. Official Annex could not be located via any channel (live URL, Wayback CDX, NAS) — reconstructed from singpromos.com's detailed per-project supply/price table, reconciling exactly to the press release's own 4,156-unit new-BTO headline (2 non-mature towns: Punggol, Yishun). A separate table of 1,137 'additional 2-Room' balance flats spread across 8 towns (Bukit Panjang, Choa Chu Kang, Hougang, Jurong East, Jurong West, Punggol, Sembawang, Woodlands) was excluded per the established convention of not seeding balance-flat supply, which isn't tied to a discrete BTO project. Two partial-combine clusters, same pattern as Boon Lay View & Yung Kuang Court (Nov 2013): (1) 'The Verandah @ Matilda & Waterway View' — Waterway View's 3-Room is its own row, but 4-Room/5-Room are jointly labeled across both projects with no further split published; (2) 'Palm Breeze @ Yishun & Saraca Breeze @ Yishun' — 3-Room/4-Room/5-Room shared between both, but 3Gen belongs to Saraca Breeze alone. Matilda Edge and Khatib Court are both standalone (no combining needed). First 3Gen flats offered in the Yishun BTO context (Saraca Breeze).
