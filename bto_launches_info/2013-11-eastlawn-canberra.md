## Curation Note

Predates HDB's Oct 2024 Standard/Plus/Prime classification framework AND predates the 2-Room Flexi scheme — flats are plain '2-room' rather than '2-room Flexi'. Official Annex could not be located via any channel (live URL, Wayback CDX, NAS) — reconstructed from singpromos.com's detailed per-project supply/price table, reconciling exactly to the press release's own 4,978-unit BTO headline (6 projects across 5 non-mature towns: Bukit Batok, Hougang, Jurong West, Sembawang, Woodlands). This exercise was combined with a Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) offering (3,974 flats, separate Annex A2) — SBF units excluded from this dataset per convention. Admiralty Grove and West Ridges @ Bukit Batok each have two 2-Room size variants (35 sqm / Type 1 and 45 sqm / Type 2), same pattern as later launches — both kept as separate flat rows. Boon Lay View & Yung Kuang Court: HDB's own summary pricing table lists 3-Room and 3Gen under 'Boon Lay View' alone, but jointly labels the 4-Room and 5-Room rows 'Boon Lay View & Yung Kuang Court'. Cross-checked against btohq.com project specs, which confirm Boon Lay View is an 810-unit standalone development (3-Room/4-Room/5-Room/3Gen, 8 blocks) and Yung Kuang Court is a 528-unit standalone development (4-Room/5-Room only, 5 blocks) — 810 + 528 = 1,338, matching this table's combined 4/5-Room + 3-Room + 3Gen total exactly, but no official per-project split of the 608 4-Room / 478 5-Room units between the two sites was found anywhere, so combined into one entry here (same pattern as Marsiling Greenview & Admiralty Flora, May 2014) rather than fabricating a split. This was HDB's largest joint BTO+SBF exercise to date (8,952 total flats), bringing 2013's full-year BTO supply to 25,139 units against a 25,000-unit commitment.
