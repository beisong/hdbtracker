# Tampines GreenRidges

- **Launch**: November 2014 BTO (`2014-11`)
- **Town**: TAMPINES
- **Classification**: Mature Town
- **Location**: Tampines North (first housing development there), Tampines Street 61. First project to offer 3Gen flats in a mature town. No 2-Room flats in this project.
- **Coordinates**: 1.361851221948341, 103.937073184127
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
- **Application window**: 2014-11-25 to 2014-12-01
