# West Edge @ Bukit Batok & West Rock @ Bukit Batok

- **Launch**: February 2015 BTO (`2015-02`)
- **Town**: BUKIT BATOK
- **Classification**: Non-Mature Town
- **Location**: Bukit Batok Road, Bukit Batok West Avenue 5/6/8. Two separately-named HDB projects (West Edge: 1,594 units of 2/3/4/5-Room; West Rock: 448 units of 4/5-Room/3Gen — first 3Gen flats ever offered in Bukit Batok) sharing one combined flat-supply/pricing table — combined into one entry here since no official per-project 4-Room/5-Room split was found.
- **Coordinates**: 1.358301939628108, 103.739753503555
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
- **Application window**: 2015-02-11 to 2015-02-17
