# Waterfront I @ Northshore

- **Launch**: November 2015 BTO (`2015-11`)
- **Town**: PUNGGOL
- **Classification**: Non-Mature Town
- **Location**: Punggol Northshore district, bounded by Northshore Drive and Punggol Reservoir. Has only the larger (45 sqm Type 2) 2-Room Flexi size, no 36 sqm units. 3-Room/4-Room/5-Room price shared with Northshore StraitsView/Waterfront II in the official price table.
- **Coordinates**: 1.416630786199498, 103.8994883393301
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
- **Application window**: 2015-11-17 to 2015-11-26
