# Clementi Peaks

- **Launch**: February 2017 BTO (`2017-02`)
- **Town**: CLEMENTI
- **Classification**: Mature Town
- **Location**: Bounded by Clementi Avenue 1 and Avenue 2. Only 2 of 4 blocks (424 of 1,104 total units) offered in this exercise — the other 2 blocks were set aside for SERS rehousing of West Coast Road residents. No 2-Room Flexi or 3Gen flats among the units offered.
- **Coordinates**: 1.311057699923095, 103.7684984892833
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
- **Application window**: 2017-02-14 to 2017-02-20
