# Anchorvale Village

- **Launch**: November 2017 BTO (`2017-11`)
- **Town**: SENGKANG
- **Classification**: Non-Mature Town
- **Location**: Bounded by Anchorvale Street and Anchorvale Road, near Farmway LRT station. No 4-Room, 5-Room or 3Gen flats in this project.
