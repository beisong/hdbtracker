# Plantation Grove

- **Launch**: November 2018 BTO (`2018-11`)
- **Town**: TENGAH
- **Classification**: Non-Mature Town
- **Location**: Plantation District, Tengah town, along Bukit Batok Road — the first public housing development in Tengah
- **Coordinates**: 1.35836009636405, 103.7368365783425
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
