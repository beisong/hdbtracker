# McNair Heights

- **Launch**: February 2021 BTO (`2021-02`)
- **Town**: KALLANG/WHAMPOA
- **Classification**: Mature Town / Estate
- **Location**: McNair Road. 2-Room Flexi flats here are offered only to seniors (55+) on short leases of 15-45 years.
- **Coordinates**: 1.31795, 103.857365
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
- **Application window**: 2021-02-04 to 2021-02-10
