# Heart of Yew Tee

- **Launch**: November 2021 BTO (`2021-11`)
- **Town**: CHOA CHU KANG
- **Classification**: Non-Mature Town
- **Location**: Yew Tee Close. 2-Room Flexi flats here are offered only to seniors (55+) on short leases of 15-45 years — no 99-year-lease option or other flat types in this project.
- **Coordinates**: 1.397394, 103.746056
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
- **Application window**: 2021-11-17 to 2021-11-23
