# Parc Clover @ Tengah

- **Launch**: November 2021 BTO (`2021-11`)
- **Town**: TENGAH
- **Classification**: Non-Mature Town
- **Location**: Tengah Drive. The 3-Room/4-Room/5-Room rows each span a floor-area range (e.g. 3-Room 70-71 sqm); the rounded midpoint is stored since Annex A gives one combined unit count per row.
- **Coordinates**: 1.360684, 103.735583
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
- **Application window**: 2021-11-17 to 2021-11-23
