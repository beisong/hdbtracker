# River Peaks I & II

- **Launch**: November 2021 BTO (`2021-11`)
- **Town**: CENTRAL AREA
- **Classification**: Prime Location Public Housing Model
- **Location**: Kelantan Road, Rochor. The first Prime Location Public Housing (PLH) project ever launched.
- **Coordinates**: 1.304984, 103.856163
- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
- **Application window**: 2021-11-17 to 2021-11-23
