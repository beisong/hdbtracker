- **Launch**: February 2022 BTO (`2022-02`)
- **Town**: KALLANG/WHAMPOA
- **Classification**: Prime Location Public Housing Model
- **Location**: King George's Avenue. Second PLH project ever launched, after River Peaks (Nov 2021).

- **Estimated waiting time**: not published for this launch
