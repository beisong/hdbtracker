# Rail Green I & II @ CCK

- **Launch**: October 2023 BTO (`2023-10`)
- **Town**: BUKIT PANJANG
- **Classification**: Non-Mature Town
- **Location**: Woodlands Road. Two phases (Rail Green I & II) sharing one flat-supply/pricing table; waiting time is 39 months for Rail Green I and 48 months for Rail Green II — the higher figure is stored here. Despite the '@ CCK' branding (proximity to Choa Chu Kang MRT), this project resolves to Bukit Panjang town by its actual HDB block coordinates, same quirk as Rail Garden @ CCK (Feb 2024). The 4-Room row spans 93-95 sqm and 5-Room spans 113-115 sqm across the two phases; midpoint values (94 sqm / 114 sqm) are stored since Annex A gives one combined unit count per row, not a per-phase split.
- **Coordinates**: 1.384408, 103.757369
- **Estimated waiting time**: 48 months
- **Application window**: 2023-10-04 to 2023-10-10
