## Curation note

Excludes 250 Community Care Apartment (CCA) units at this project (34 sqm, 30-year lease, $80,000-$95,000 indicative) — CCAs are not standard BTO flats and are omitted from flats[]. This launch's headline total of 6,057 flats includes these 250 CCA units; the sum of this launch's flats[] units is 5,807. Annex A lists waiting time as '39 / 43' months (varies by flat type) — the higher figure is stored here.
